Privacy center

A clear look at what this prototype does with your data.

◈

Local-first storage

Profiles, posts, likes, and the signed-in username are stored in your browser using localStorage. They are not synced between devices.

◎

No tracking scripts

This single-file prototype includes no analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party JavaScript.

▤

Posts are rendered as text

User-written post content is inserted with textContent rather than interpreted as HTML.

⌫

You can clear your content

Use “Delete my posts” on your profile, or clear this site’s browser storage to remove all locally saved app data.

Prototype limitation: passwords are stored in plain text in localStorage. Do not use a real or reused password. A production app needs secure server-side authentication and password hashing. Local storage is not a secure vault.
